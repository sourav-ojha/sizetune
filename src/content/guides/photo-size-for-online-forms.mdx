Many online forms limit uploaded photos to a size in kilobytes (KB). This guide explains what that number means and how to meet it.

## What "KB" means for a photo

File size depends on pixel dimensions and on compression. A phone photo is often 2,000–5,000 KB; a form may ask for 20–200 KB.

## How to reach the limit

1. Start from the original image, not a screenshot of it.
2. Lower the JPEG quality first; reduce dimensions only if needed.
3. Check the final size before uploading.
