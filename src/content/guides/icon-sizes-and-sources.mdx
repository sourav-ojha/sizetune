Favicon and app icon sizes are repeated on many websites, often with different numbers and often out of date. SizeTune's favicon generator lists each file it makes next to the reason for its size. This page gives the official pages those reasons come from, what each one says, and when we last read it, so you can check a number yourself.

*Published and last updated 17 November 2026 by SizeTune.*

## How the sizes are chosen

A size is marked as official only when a page from the platform or a standards body states it: Google, MDN, web.dev, Android Developers or Apple. Where a size is common practice but we found no official page stating it, the generator says so instead of presenting it as a rule. The 16, 32 and 48 px images packed inside favicon.ico and the 180 by 180 px Apple touch icon are of that kind.

## Why the dates matter

Icon guidance changes: the Apple page we read lists a change-log entry on app icon formats dated October 28, 2026, and platforms revise their numbers from time to time. If a source looks old to you, open it and compare, and where the numbers differ, trust the platform.
