## Summary
Fixed-KB queries ("resize image to 20kb") are crowded: big converters (Zamzar, Cloudinary) mix with dozens of clone tool pages. [E: inference; medium; S-001; 2026-11-19] Signature-size queries show signNow programmatic pages plus small tools. [E: inference; medium; S-002; 2026-11-19] Exam-specific queries (SSC, IBPS, GDS, PAN) are won by one-page-per-exam sites, and their stated specs conflict. [E: inference; medium; S-004; 2026-11-19] PDF-under-X-KB queries are held by vendor suites and SizeTune has no PDF tool. [E: inference; medium; S-006; 2026-11-19] No competitor traffic is known.

## SERP observations
All served from the US via a search tool; India was not observed. Autocomplete confirms the phrasing exists (20, 100, 50, 30, 40, 15 KB; GDS, RRB, PAN, SSC, IBPS signature queries; "increase image size in kb"; "jpg to pdf under 200kb"). [E: fact; high; S-019; 2026-11-19] I re-fetched four autocomplete lists and read the 25 KB, 15 KB and 50 KB competitor pages myself; they matched. [E: fact; high; S-029; 2026-11-19]
- "resize image to 20kb/50kb/100kb": Zamzar, Cloudinary, imresizer, clone pages; large platforms present. [E: inference; medium; S-001; 2026-11-19]
- "reduce signature size to 10kb" and "resize signature to 20kb": signNow holds about 12 of 20 US results with programmatic 20kb pages (reviewer check), small exam tools fill the rest. [E: inference; medium; S-007; 2026-11-19]
- "kb to mb converter": calculator brands and a featured answer. [E: inference; medium; S-013; 2026-11-19]
- "increase image size in kb": mid-size tools, no giant. [E: inference; medium; S-017; 2026-11-19]
- Conflicting exam specs across third-party sites: not verified against official notices. [E: unknown; high; search:official exam notices not fetched; 2026-11-19]
