## Summary
Best-documented fits are Hacker News Show HN (try-it-without-signup suits SizeTune) and AlternativeTo (free suggestion, nofollow). Reddit, Quora, Stack Exchange and exam forums look relevant but their rules could not be read, so they stay unverified. Directories and backlinks are the slow lever; competition among no-upload tools is heavy. No traffic or signup prediction is made.

## Directories
- AlternativeTo: free "suggest new application"; email-verified account needed; approval not guaranteed; links nofollow; no using profiles to advertise. [E: fact; low; S-047; 2026-11-19] (agent-reported; my fetch returned no text)
- Product Hunt: free. [E: fact; low; S-049; 2026-11-19]
- Toolify and other tool lists: rules and cost unverified. [E: unknown; low; search:Toolify page blocked; 2026-11-19]
- Avoid paid or low-quality directories: Google lists them as link spam. [E: fact; low; S-055; 2026-11-19]
- Submissions need the user's approval and are done by a person.

## Backlinks and partnerships
- "Free no-upload image tool" roundups on tool blogs and DEV.to are inclusion candidates; none contacted or confirmed. [E: inference; low; S-056; 2026-11-19]
- Exam-help blogs and college career pages could cite a per-exam spec guide only if it solves a concrete need. [E: hypothesis; low; n/a; 2026-11-19]
- No GitHub or open-source link on the site, so open-source lists do not apply now. [E: fact; medium; S-036; 2026-11-19]
- Never buy or swap links; paid links need nofollow or sponsored. [E: fact; low; S-055; 2026-11-19]

## Risks
- A new brand account posting links is the profile spam filters flag; never use fake or multiple accounts or vote requests.
- Product Hunt needs a personal identity, against brand-first anonymity.
- Earlier `.product/directories.md` assumed no account for AlternativeTo; the FAQ says an account is needed. [E: fact; low; S-058; 2026-11-19]
- "Nothing is uploaded" must stay true; users can check the network tab.
- Many near-identical no-upload exam resizers exist. [E: fact; low; S-004; 2026-11-19]
