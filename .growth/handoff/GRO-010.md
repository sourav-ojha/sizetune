# GRO-010: Add KB preset pages 30 KB and 15 KB, then 200 KB if indexed

## Goal
Autocomplete lists 30, 40, 15 and 200 KB; competitors have dedicated pages for 15 and 25 KB. Add only after the first batch shows indexing, each with unique guidance, to avoid doorway pages. Approved growth roadmap item (weeks 9-13).

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given earlier new pages are indexed, When 30 and 15 KB pages are added, Then each has its own use cases and FAQ
- AC2: Given the sitemap, When built, Then they are listed and linked from related tools

## Non-goals
- Nothing beyond this item. No publishing, posting, directory submissions, account creation or ads changes unless an acceptance criterion says so.

## Constraints and assumptions
- Evidence: inference, medium confidence. Sources: S-019 https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&q=resize%20image%20to%20; S-029 https://imageonline.io/compress-image-to-50kb/; S-030 https://imresizer.com/resize-image-to-50kb; S-005 https://www.google.com/search?q=compress+jpg+to+200kb+online.
- Target keywords: resize image to 30kb, resize image to 15kb, compress jpg to 200kb.
- Risk: Do not ship if earlier pages are not getting indexed
