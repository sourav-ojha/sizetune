# GRO-006: Make preset pages (20/50/100 KB, signature 10 KB) unique and useful

## Goal
These four pages are presets of one tool and risk thin or doorway judgement (also for ad approval). Give each its own spec guidance, before/after example, common-form notes and FAQ; do not add FAQPage markup. Approved growth roadmap item (weeks 1-4).

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given any of the four preset pages, When its text is compared to the others, Then it has at least a section of unique guidance not shared with the others
- AC2: Given each page, When read, Then it states what the limit is typically used for without claiming exam specs as fact
- AC3: Given verify:html near-duplicate checks, When run, Then they pass

## Non-goals
- Nothing beyond this item. No publishing, posting, directory submissions, account creation or ads changes unless an acceptance criterion says so.
