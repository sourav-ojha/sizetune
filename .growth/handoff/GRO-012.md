# GRO-012: Prepare AdSense readiness: contact page, consent tool, policy copy, ad slots away from the tool

## Goal
No contact page (404), no consent tool, no ads.txt, privacy copy promises an update first. Owner allows ads on tool pages but never adjacent to the tool UI. Do not add ads.txt or ad code before an ad account exists. Approved growth roadmap item (weeks 5-8).

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given the privacy page says tools are meant to stay free of ads, When ads are planned on tool pages, Then the owner has approved changing that sentence and the page is updated before any tool-page slot ships (otherwise ads stay on guides only)
- AC2: Given the site, When visited, Then a contact page exists and is linked in the footer
- AC3: Given the privacy and terms pages, When updated, Then they describe ads, cookies and consent accurately
- AC4: Given a visitor in a consent region, When the consent tool is installed, Then ads and analytics wait for consent
- AC5: Given any tool page, When ad slots are defined, Then none is placed adjacent to the tool controls or result

## Non-goals
- Nothing beyond this item. No publishing, posting, directory submissions, account creation or ads changes unless an acceptance criterion says so.

## Constraints and assumptions
- Evidence: fact, high confidence. Sources: S-039 https://sizetune.com/ads.txt; S-038 https://sizetune.com/privacy/; S-045 repo:.product/PRODUCT.md.
- Risk: Ads can hurt page speed and layout shift; start conservative
- Risk: Requires a Google-certified CMP choice by the owner
- Risk: Public promise "tools meant to stay free of ads" on the privacy page conflicts with tool-page ads until the owner changes it
- Design is open: explore it with the brainstorming skill before implementing.
