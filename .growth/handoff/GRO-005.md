# GRO-005: Add /resize-signature-to-20kb and signature KB presets

## Goal
Autocomplete shows "signature resize to 20 kb" first and the SERP has only small tools, no big platform. SizeTune has a 10 KB signature page and a signature tool but no 20 KB page. Cover 15/30/50 KB as presets on the tool, not separate pages. Approved growth roadmap item (weeks 1-4).

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given /resize-signature-to-20kb, When it loads, Then the signature tool opens preset to 20 KB with its own heading, title, copy and FAQ distinct from the 10 KB page
- AC2: Given the signature tool, When the user picks 15, 30 or 50 KB, Then the target updates without a new page
- AC3: Given the sitemap, When built, Then the new page is listed and verify:html passes

## Non-goals
- Nothing beyond this item. No publishing, posting, directory submissions, account creation or ads changes unless an acceptance criterion says so.

## Constraints and assumptions
- Evidence: inference, medium confidence. Sources: S-007 https://www.google.com/search?q=resize+signature+to+20kb+for+online+form; S-002 https://www.google.com/search?q=reduce+signature+size+to+10kb+online; S-027 https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&q=signature%20resize%20to%20; S-033 repo:src/pages.
- Target keywords: signature resize to 20 kb, signature resize to 30 kb, signature resize tool.
- Risk: Near-duplicate of the 10 KB page; needs unique copy (when to use 10 vs 20 KB, examples)
